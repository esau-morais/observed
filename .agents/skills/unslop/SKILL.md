# Plain writing

Read the whole passage. Revise without changing technical meaning.

- State what the software does, what evidence shows, and what remains unknown.
- Remove praise, chatbot openings, vague authority, marketing filler, and generic conclusions.
- Use ordinary verbs and consistent terms. Replace metaphors with mechanisms.
- Split dense sentences. Preserve qualifications defining scope and uncertainty.
- Use sentence case headings and selective emphasis.
- Use lists for parallel items and tables for comparisons. Do not force a fixed number of bullets.
- Avoid em dashes, decorative emojis, curly quotes, and rhetorical contrast formulas.
- Preserve commands, identifiers, paths, quotations, and source links. Concision must not create ambiguity.

Read again for unsupported claims and repeated instructions. Delete duplication rather than rephrasing it elsewhere.
